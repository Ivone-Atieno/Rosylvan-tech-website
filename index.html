Our story

Technology that grows with the people we serve

Rosylvan Tech started in Siaya with a simple belief: businesses, schools and organisations here deserve the same quality of technology as anywhere else, delivered by someone they can reach directly.

My passion for technology grew from seeing how much it can transform lives and businesses. I recognised a need for accessible, reliable and affordable tech solutions, from IT support and connectivity to digital skills and business systems. I started Rosylvan Tech to bridge that gap, helping individuals and organisations embrace technology with confidence.

Today we help clients secure their premises, connect their teams, get online and keep their systems running, so they can focus on growing. We build, you scale.

“Your challenges matter, your goals inspire us, and delivering solutions that help you grow is our commitment.”
